  <h2>About me</h2>

  <p>I’m a PhD student at the University of Florida studying machine learning, with a focus on theory, identifiability, and representation learning.</p>

  <p>My work centers on understanding when latent structure can be recovered from data and how that insight can support more reliable and interpretable methods in modern ML.</p>

  <p>I’m especially interested in nonlinear blind source separation, generative models, and the mathematical foundations that make learning systems robust and trustworthy.</p>
